01

01 — The challenge

The "technique plateau"

Amateur athletes hit a wall because they lack objective data. We identified three core friction points.

a

Subjectivity

"Does my form look right?" Feedback relies on the naked eye, missing micro-movements and velocity changes.

b

Accessibility

Professional biomechanics labs cost thousands of dollars. Elite data was gated behind elite paywalls.

c

Data overload

Existing trackers show the "what" (heart rate: 150) but fail to explain the "how" (knee valgus angle: 12°).

Source analysis

Simplifying kinetic chain data

Performance tracking means analyzing the kinetic chain — the sequence of body segments creating motion. The challenge was displaying complex joint angles, velocity vectors and force distribution in a simple, non-overwhelming way.

The system must instantly translate this dense technical information into a clear, actionable Performance Score — the core goal of the UI design.
